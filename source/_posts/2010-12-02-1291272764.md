GUIDE TO A BETTER LIFE - DR RANDY PAUSCH

He was a Carnegie-Mellon professor and was in his forties.

Died of pancreatic cancer in 2008, but wrote a book ‘The last lecture” ,
one of the bestsellers in 2007.  What a legacy to leave  behind…
In a letter to his wife Jai and  his children, Dylan, Logan , and Chloe, he
wrote this beautiful  "guide to a better life" for them  to follow.
May you be  blessed by his insight.

POINTS  ON HOW TO IMPROVE YOUR LIFE

Personality:
1. Don't  compare your life to others'. You have no idea what their journey
is all  about.
2. Don't have negative thoughts of things you cannot control. Instead 
invest your energy in the positive present moment
3. Don't over do; keep  your limits
4. Don't take yourself so seriously; no one else does
5.  Don't waste your precious energy on gossip
6. Dream more while you are awake   
7. Envy is a waste of time. You already have all you need..
8.  Forget issues of the past. Don't remind your partner of his/her mistakes
of the  past. That will ruin your present happiness.
9. Life is too short to waste  time hating anyone. Don't hate others.
10. Make peace with your past so it  won't spoil the present
11. No one is in charge of your happiness except you 
12. Realize that life is a school and you are here to learn.
Problems are simply part of the curriculum that appear and fade away like 
algebra class but the lessons you learn will last a lifetime.
13. Smile and  laugh more
14. You don't have to win every argument. Agree to  disagree.

Community:
15. Call your  family often
16. Each day give something good to others
17. Forgive  everyone for everything
18. Spend time with people over the age of 70 &  under the age of 6
19. Try to make at least three people smile each day 
20. What other people think of you is none of your business
21. Your job  will not take care of you when you are sick. Your family and
friends will. Stay  in touch.
